# Epic: Island Empire MVP

> **Slug**: island-empire
> **Status**: In Progress
> **Layer**: Foundation & Core
> **Source**: `design/game-brief.md`

## Goal
Build the initial vertical slice of Island Empire: a 3D RTS/city-builder on Godot 4.7 featuring 3D island environments, touch/mouse camera controls, resource gathering, building construction, squad combat, naval transport, and Stone-to-Bronze Age progression.

## Scope (from Game Brief)
1. Island terrain & touch/mouse RTS camera rig (pan, zoom, rotate).
2. Resource nodes & villager gathering economy (wood, stone, food, gold).
3. Building placement grid & construction system (Town Center, House, Farm, Lumber Camp, Barracks, Harbor).
4. Unit training & tactical squad combat (Villager, Spearman, Archer).
5. Harbor, naval transport & sea traversal to second island.
6. Enemy island camp & automated amphibious raiding parties.
7. Age advancement (Stone → Bronze) & persistent state / HUD.
